# Wallet — your cards, events and contacts

[`wallet.dotrino.com`](https://wallet.dotrino.com/) · repo
[`dotrino-wallet`](https://github.com/imdotrino/dotrino-wallet)

Wallet puts in one place three things that normally float around your inbox:
**events**, **contacts** and **passes** (boarding passes, tickets, coupons). Each
one becomes a card, sorted into tabs.

## How something gets in

- **Import a file**: `.ics` (event), `.vcf` (contact) or `.pkpass` (pass). On
  Android, with the app installed, you can open the file straight from your mail
  and choose Wallet.
- **Create it by hand** in the app.

## What you can do with a card

- Edit it.
- **Share it** by QR code or link.
- **Export it** back to its original format, to open it in your calendar, address
  book or phone wallet.

## Where it lives

In [your own store](/en/empezar/privacidad/), not in someone else's account. A
plane ticket or your home address are exactly the kind of thing that has no
business sitting on anyone's server.
