# Android Launcher

[`android-launcher.dotrino.com`](https://android-launcher.dotrino.com/) · repo
[`dotrino-android-launcher`](https://github.com/imdotrino/dotrino-android-launcher)

It is your phone's home screen, swapped for a clean list.

## What it does

- **All your apps from A to Z**, with a letter index down the side to jump fast.
- **Hide apps.** The ones you choose stop showing in the list and only open after
  your **fingerprint or pattern**.
- No ads, no suggestions and no keeping count of what you open.

## Installing it

Download the install file (`.apk`) from the
[app's page](https://android-launcher.dotrino.com/) and install it like any other.
Android will ask for permission to install from outside the store: that is
expected, and the file carries its version in the name so you know which one you
have.

Then, the first time you press the home button, the phone asks which home screen
to use. Pick Dotrino Launcher and tick **Always**.
