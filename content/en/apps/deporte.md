# Sport: scoreboards and timer

## Ecuavóley scoreboard

[`ecuavoley.dotrino.com`](https://ecuavoley.dotrino.com/) · repo
[`dotrino-ecuavoley-contador`](https://github.com/imdotrino/dotrino-ecuavoley-contador)

A scoreboard for the court: two big panels, one per team. Tap a panel and the
point goes up. It has a **serve** indicator, **undo** (for the point that was not),
**switch sides** and **reset**. First to 15 wins.

## Padel scoreboard

[`padel.dotrino.com`](https://padel.dotrino.com/) · repo
[`dotrino-padel-contador`](https://github.com/imdotrino/dotrino-padel-contador)

The same thing with tennis scoring: 0/15/30/40, games and sets, **tie-break** and
**golden point** if you turn it on. Serve, undo and reset as well.

Both work offline: on a court with no signal they keep counting all the same.

## GymBro — interval timer

[`gymbro.dotrino.com`](https://gymbro.dotrino.com/) · repo
[`dotrino-gymbro`](https://github.com/imdotrino/dotrino-gymbro)

Set how long you work, how long you rest, how many rounds and a countdown to get
ready. It cues you with **sound** and **vibration**, keeps cueing with the screen
off, and keeps the screen awake while you train. Works offline.

## World Cup 2026

Two apps tied to one tournament, today **out of the catalogue** but still
published:

- **Results** — [`mundial.dotrino.com`](https://mundial.dotrino.com/) · repo
  [`dotrino-mundial`](https://github.com/imdotrino/dotrino-mundial): fixtures,
  groups and table, from a signed results feed.
- **Predictions** — [`pronostico.dotrino.com`](https://pronostico.dotrino.com/) ·
  repo
  [`dotrino-pronostico-mundialista`](https://github.com/imdotrino/dotrino-pronostico-mundialista):
  build your bracket and compare it with your friends.

They are kept around to be reused for another tournament.
