# The games

They all open in the browser, can be [installed](/en/empezar/instalar-apps/), work
offline (the single-player ones) and **ask for no account**. Your progress lives on
your device.

## Two players or more

### Chess

[`chess.dotrino.com`](https://chess.dotrino.com/) · repo
[`dotrino-chess`](https://github.com/imdotrino/dotrino-chess)

Chess online. Create a **public** game (anyone sees it in the lobby and joins) or a
**private** one (you pass the link to whoever you want) and play in real time.

### Cuarenta

[`cuarenta.dotrino.com`](https://cuarenta.dotrino.com/) · repo
[`dotrino-cuarenta`](https://github.com/imdotrino/dotrino-cuarenta)

Ecuador's traditional card game, for **2 or 4 players**, with the rules everybody
grew up with.

### GridGame

[`gridgame.dotrino.com`](https://gridgame.dotrino.com/) · repo
[`dotrino-gridgame`](https://github.com/imdotrino/dotrino-gridgame)

**In development.** A cooperative world on a grid: each player builds their own
surroundings and explores an endless world with programmable props, characters and
enemies.

## Single player

### Sudoku

[`sudoku.dotrino.com`](https://sudoku.dotrino.com/) · repo
[`dotrino-sudoku`](https://github.com/imdotrino/dotrino-sudoku)

Sudoku with a **level adventure**: a map with branching paths and **bosses** that
open with **stars** (1 to 3 per level depending on how you solve it). Four
difficulties, a **daily challenge**, pencil notes, undo and a timer.

Every level is the **same for everyone**, so if you share one your friend plays
exactly that challenge — and sharing earns you **a hint** to spend.

### Critters

[`critters.dotrino.com`](https://critters.dotrino.com/) · repo
[`dotrino-critters`](https://github.com/imdotrino/dotrino-critters)

Collectible monsters that fight on their own. You build the lineup, give each
critter its role (attack, defence or support) and who to target first; the battle
resolves itself. Strategy wins, not reflexes. You can **fuse** critters to evolve,
reinforce or devolve them, and the campaign never ends.

### Diamonds

[`diamonds.dotrino.com`](https://diamonds.dotrino.com/) · repo
[`dotrino-diamonds`](https://github.com/imdotrino/dotrino-diamonds)

A matching game: swap neighbouring gems to line up three or more of the same
colour and chain cascading combos. Simple, for a spare minute.

### Trivia

[`trivia.dotrino.com`](https://trivia.dotrino.com/) · repo
[`dotrino-trivia`](https://github.com/imdotrino/dotrino-trivia)

Trivia **you build**. Paste your questions as a `JSON` file (the app comes with a
ready-made prompt if you would rather have an AI write them), pick one colour that
derives the whole palette, add your logo and background, and publish a clean link
for people to play. There is a scored quiz mode, true/false and flashcards.
