# Eco — posting in your area

[`eco.dotrino.com`](https://eco.dotrino.com/) · repo
[`dotrino-eco`](https://github.com/imdotrino/dotrino-eco)

Eco is speaking up **where you are**. You write an eco —text, a link, tags, an
image— and it is discovered by whoever has you inside their **listening radius**.
After **24 hours it goes quiet**: it stops existing on the network.

## How to use it

1. Open the app and allow location (it is used to know which area you post to).
2. Set your **listening radius**: how far around you want to hear.
3. Write and post. Your eco shows up for whoever is close by.

## Ephemeral is the normal case

An eco is not stored on any server. It lives while it lasts, on the devices that
received it, and then it is gone. That is what the app promises, which is why
there is no history to browse and no archive for anyone to dig through later.

## Keeping a copy (optional, one eco at a time)

If an eco matters to you and you want to keep it, you can ask for **your own copy**
to be stored in your own content store —your computer, not anyone's cloud. It is
decided **eco by eco** and always by you, because keeping something changes what
the app promises. With nothing running, everything works the same: ephemeral is
still what happens by default.

See [How your data is kept private](/en/empezar/privacidad/) and
