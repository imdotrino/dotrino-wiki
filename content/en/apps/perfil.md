# Profile and ratings

## Profile — who you are on Dotrino

[`profile.dotrino.com`](https://profile.dotrino.com/) · repo
[`dotrino-profile-app`](https://github.com/imdotrino/dotrino-profile-app)

This is where [your identity](/en/empezar/identidad/) is managed. From here:

- **Your profiles.** You can have several on the same device (a personal one and a
  work one, say) and choose which is active. Every other app uses the active one.
- **Your details.** Name, photo and whichever fields you want to fill in. Each one
  is your choice, and some —like phone or address— start out hidden.
- **Your PIN.** Protects the profile on this device.
- **Your vault.** Connect the profile to [your vault](/en/vault/modelo/) and review
  [which devices are linked](/en/vault/emparejar/).

The round button at the top right of every app opens your profile card; to **edit
it or switch profiles** it brings you here, the only place where that changes.

## Reputation — rating and asking

[`reputation.dotrino.com`](https://reputation.dotrino.com/) · repo
[`dotrino-reputation-app`](https://github.com/imdotrino/dotrino-reputation-app)

Leave your opinion about a person, a website or an account on another network, and
see what **your trusted network** thinks.

The idea behind it: **what people you trust say carries weight**. A flood of
opinions from accounts nobody knows does not move the needle, so manufacturing a
good name with fresh accounts does not work.

It also has questions and answers about a subject: answers rise by how much their
author weighs for your network, not by how many clicks they collected.
