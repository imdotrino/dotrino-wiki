# Trueque — listings near you

[`trueque.dotrino.com`](https://trueque.dotrino.com/) · repo
[`dotrino-trueque`](https://github.com/imdotrino/dotrino-trueque)

Trueque is the neighbourhood board: **selling**, **giving away**, **looking for**.
Listings belong to whoever is nearby and **expire after 24 hours**.

## How to use it

1. Open the app and allow location.
2. Set the **radius**: how far around you want to look.
3. Post your listing, or use the **proximity radar** to see what is out there.
4. When something catches your eye, message whoever posted it and close the deal.

## Why it expires

An old listing helps nobody, and a board that never forgets turns into a record of
your movements. Here the listing switches itself off and **your location is not
stored**: it is used to tell whether something is inside your radius, and that is
all.
