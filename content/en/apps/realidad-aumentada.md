# AR — your works come alive

[`ar.dotrino.com`](https://ar.dotrino.com/) · repo
[`dotrino-ar`](https://github.com/imdotrino/dotrino-ar)

You point your phone's camera at a painting, a poster or a photo, and an image or a
video appears on top of it, staying attached to the work as you move the phone.

**It is in development.** Today works are seen on the same phone that added them; they
cannot be shared with someone else yet.

## Adding a work

1. Open the **My works** tab.
2. In **Photo of the work**, choose a photo taken from the front, well lit and without
   reflections.
3. In **What shows on top**, choose an image or a video.
4. Give it a name and press **Add**.

The phone prepares the work for a few seconds. The photo never leaves it.

## Viewing it

1. Open the **View** tab and press **Open the camera**.
2. The first time, the browser asks for permission to use the camera.
3. Point at the work. Once it is recognized, its name shows at the bottom and the content
   is drawn on top.

Videos start without sound. The **Sound off** button turns it on.

## Which works are recognized well

| Recognized well | Harder |
|---|---|
| lots of detail: textures, strokes, text, sharp edges | flat colors, soft gradients |
| matte surface | glass or varnish with reflections |
| even light | dim light or a direct spotlight |

The image or video is stretched to the size of the work: it helps if it has the same
proportions.

## Where each thing is kept

- **The list of your works** goes in your storage, with your profile.
- **The photo and what shows on top** stay on that phone. If you clear the browser's
  data, the work has to be added again.
