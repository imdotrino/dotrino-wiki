# here — your live location

[`here.dotrino.com`](https://here.dotrino.com/) · repo
[`dotrino-here`](https://github.com/imdotrino/dotrino-here)

here does the usual thing: letting your family know you are almost there. The
difference is what it does **not** do: it keeps no record of where you have been.

## Circles

You share with **circles** —family, friends, Saturday's team—, not with the world.
Each circle is end-to-end encrypted: the server only ever sees sealed data it
cannot open.

When you invite someone into a circle you hand them a permission signed from your
identity. That permission **expires** and you can **take it back** whenever you
want; from then on they stop seeing you.

## How to use it

1. Open the app and create a circle.
2. Share the invitation with whoever should see you.
3. So your location keeps updating with the app closed, set up **OwnTracks** (the
   app that does the background tracking) following the instructions here shows you.

## What is not there

- **No history.** No trail is stored for anyone to look at afterwards.
- **No proximity discovery.** Nobody finds you by standing next to you; the people
  in your circle see you, and nobody else.
