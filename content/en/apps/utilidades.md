# Utilities

Three of those tools you use once and they save the day. All three do their work
**inside your browser**: what you paste or upload never leaves your device.

## Base64

[`base64.dotrino.com`](https://base64.dotrino.com/) · repo
[`dotrino-base64`](https://github.com/imdotrino/dotrino-base64)

Converts **text and files** to Base64 and back. It handles accents and emoji
(Unicode) and has the **URL-safe** variant. You can encode a file to embed it
somewhere, or paste Base64 and **download it as a file**.

## My IP

[`myip.dotrino.com`](https://myip.dotrino.com/) · repo
[`dotrino-myip`](https://github.com/imdotrino/dotrino-myip)

Tells you your **public address** on the internet and the details of your
connection: country, where you leave the network, whether it is encrypted, whether
you are on a VPN, the connection type and how long it takes. It also detects your
device's address inside your home network.

Everything is measured against Dotrino's own infrastructure: no outside service is
asked what your address is.

## Favicon Generator

[`favicon.dotrino.com`](https://favicon.dotrino.com/) · repo
[`dotrino-favicon-generator`](https://github.com/imdotrino/dotrino-favicon-generator)

Upload a PNG or JPG and get back the **favicon** —the little tab icon— in every
size that is needed, including the `.ico` Windows asks for, ready for your site or
your app.
