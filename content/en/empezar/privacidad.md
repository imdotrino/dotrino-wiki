# How your data is kept private

There is a single principle: **the information is yours, and so are the decisions
about it** — what you share, with whom, when and why. This is what the ecosystem does
to make that literal.

## What the apps do not carry

- **No ads and no cookies.**
- **No third-party JavaScript**: no app loads code from an ad network or an outside
  measurement service.
- **No record of what you do**: the only measurement is our own aggregate counter,
  hosted on our server, counting page visits — not people.

## Where your content lives

Your things are stored **on your device** and sync encrypted if you turn that on. A
Dotrino app opens and works without asking any server for anything: that is why it
keeps responding offline.

## What you share, when you share it

Shareable content travels after the `#` in the address — the part the browser
**never sends to the server**. It is neither stored nor indexable: it exists between
you and the person you gave the link to. Details in
[How what you share travels](/en/empezar/como-viaja/).

## The honest limit

**No app can look after what its owner decides to show.** From that point on your
decision rules, not the code. That is why the design aims for sharing to always be an
explicit, reversible act, never a box ticked in advance.

## It can be checked

All the code is open, MIT licensed, at [`imdotrino`](https://github.com/imdotrino):
read it, build it, run its tests. What is claimed here can be verified.
