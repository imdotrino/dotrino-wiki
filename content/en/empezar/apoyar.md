# Supporting the project

Dotrino is **not funded by ads or by selling data**, and it does not charge you for
using the apps. It is sustained another way.

## Chip in

A monthly contribution on [Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/dotrino) sustains the project
and, depending on the tier, earns you **a voice in the roadmap**: seeing and
suggesting what gets worked on, or having your ideas taken first. The coin in every
app's top bar leads there.

## Contribute

All the code is open (MIT). Report a problem, propose an app, translate, or send a
pull request: see [Contributing](/en/desarrollo/contribuir/).

## Stay close

The project's conversation happens on [Discord](https://discord.gg/D648uq7cth): apps
get suggested there, the roadmap is visible and questions get answered.

## And if you need something built

Custom software is also developed and deployed, and that funds the time invested in
the ecosystem. Write to us from the contact form at
[dotrino.com](https://dotrino.com/).
