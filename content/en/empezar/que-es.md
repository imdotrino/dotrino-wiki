# What is Dotrino

Dotrino is an **ecosystem of applications centered on data privacy**. One principle
orders everything: the information is yours, and so are the decisions about it —
what you share, with whom, when and why.

That is not an abstraction, it is a concrete inventory: your voice, your face, the
way you write, your conversations, your photos, where you are, who you talk to, what
you search for, what you buy, your identity, your history, your documents.

## Your data, on your server, under your rules

Your photos, your messages and your files live where you put them, and you can take
them with you, copy them or delete them any day you want. What you don't share never
moves from there.

## How it works while you use it

This is what the apps do for you without you configuring anything:

- **You start by opening.** You open the app and that is it: you are using it from the
  first moment, with no sign-up. See [How to create an account](/en/empezar/crear-cuenta/).
- **From your machine to theirs.** When the connection allows it, what you send travels
  from one machine to the other by the shortest path.
- **Your message waits.** If the other person is away, your message waits for them for
  up to a day and arrives the moment they come back. See
  [How what you share travels](/en/empezar/como-viaja/).
- **What's yours comes along.** Your contacts and your messages are the same in every
  Dotrino app you open in that browser.
- **It carries your signature.** What you send goes out with a signature born on your
  machine, so whoever receives it knows it came from you. See
  [Your identity and your profiles](/en/empezar/identidad/).
- **Nearby, when you want.** Some apps help you see what is around you. Each time that
  happens, you decide, and it clears itself within hours. See
  [How your data is kept private](/en/empezar/privacidad/).

## How it is guaranteed

- The applications share **identity, transport and encrypted storage**, under the MIT
  license, with no ads, no cookies and no third-party JavaScript.
- **The key lives on your device.** You are your own certifier: you enroll your
  devices and sign for yourself, without asking any central gatekeeper.
- **Your content lives in your store**, encrypted end to end.
- Anything shareable travels in the URL *fragment* (`#…`), which **never reaches the
  server** and cannot be indexed.

## The pieces

| Piece | What it does |
|---|---|
| [The vault](/en/vault/modelo/) | your personal certifier and store, on your machine |
| The apps (`*.dotrino.com`) | solve real problems — messaging, location, games… — without tracking you |
| The pillars (`@dotrino/*`) | identity, transport, storage, reputation: libraries every app shares |
