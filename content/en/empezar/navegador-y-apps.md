# The browser and installed apps

Some Dotrino apps exist in two forms on your phone: **in the browser** (Chrome,
Safari) and **as an app installed from the store** (Google Play, App Store). Both
keep your identity, but **each in its own place**, and that has a consequence worth
knowing from the start.

## The most important part

**Profiles in the browser don't show up in installed apps, and profiles in installed
apps don't show up in the browser.** Neither can read what the other keeps. This is
not a bug: the phone does not let a browser and an app look inside each other.

| | Where your profiles are kept | Who sees them |
|---|---|---|
| **Browser** (Chrome, Safari…) | inside that browser | every Dotrino app you open **in that browser** |
| **Installed apps on Android** | in the **Dotrino Identity** app | every Dotrino app **installed** on that phone |
| **Installed apps on iPhone/iPad** | in a space the Dotrino apps share | every Dotrino app **installed** on that phone |

Put another way: on one phone you can have **two groups** that don't know each other.
The browser's, and the installed apps'.

## Dotrino Identity: an app on Android, an idea on iPhone

**On Android** there is an app called **Dotrino Identity**. It does nothing on its
own: it keeps your keys and profiles so the other installed Dotrino apps can share
them. Dotrino's Android apps need it; if it's missing, they explain why and take you
to Google Play.

**On iPhone and iPad that app doesn't exist, and isn't needed.** The identity is still
there: Apple lets apps from the same maker share a saved space, so each Dotrino app
keeps your profiles there and the others see them. There is nothing to install. If
you search the App Store for "Dotrino Identity" and it doesn't show up, that's why.

Either way the result is the same: **switch profile, create one or sign in on one
installed app, and every other installed app on that phone sees it**. And either way,
the browser stays separate.

## How to use the same profile in the browser and in the apps

Since they don't see each other, you don't move a profile from one side to the other
by copying it. You do the same as with a different device, because **to your account
they are different devices**: the browser is one, the installed apps are another.

- **Connect them to your vault.** From the side that doesn't have the profile, open
  the round button's menu and choose **Adopt a profile**. The steps are in
  [how to use your account on other devices](/en/empezar/otros-dispositivos/).
- **Sign in** with your address (`name@AB12-CD34-EF56`) and your password, if your
  account has one. It's also in the round button's menu.

After that it's **the same account** on both sides: the same name, the same profile,
the same contacts, and your things in sync. Your vault's device list will show **two
devices** for that phone: the browser and the installed apps.

## If you do nothing

Each side keeps its own account and everything works the same. They are just
different accounts: what you do in the browser doesn't show up in the installed app,
and the other way around.
