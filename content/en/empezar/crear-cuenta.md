# How to create an account

**You don't have to create one.** Dotrino has no sign-up, no confirmation email and
no password to invent. The account **is created by itself the first time you open an
app**: your browser generates a key for you, right there, and that key is your
account.

That key **never leaves your device** and nobody else has it — not even us.

## The only part you do: your details

The account starts empty, with a provisional name. Whenever you feel like it:

1. Open any Dotrino app, for example [dotrino.com](https://dotrino.com/).
2. Tap the **round button** in the top bar (the one with your picture).
3. Go to [profile.dotrino.com](https://profile.dotrino.com/) and write your name,
   your picture and whatever details you want to show.

You decide what is visible: phone and address, for instance, start hidden. You can
change or hide anything later.

## It is the same account in every app

You don't create one account per app. The key is the same, so **your contacts and
your things follow you** from one app to another in that browser.

## You can have several

One device can hold **several profiles** — a personal one and a work one, say — each
with its own key and its own things, never mixed. They are managed at
[profile.dotrino.com](https://profile.dotrino.com/).

## Careful: clearing your browser data deletes it

Because the key lives in your browser, **clearing the site's data deletes the
account**, and there is no way to get it back. If that account matters to you, the
next step is having it in more than one place:
[how to use your account on other devices](/en/empezar/otros-dispositivos/).
