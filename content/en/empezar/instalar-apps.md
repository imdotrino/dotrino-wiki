# Install an app on your phone

Dotrino apps open **in the browser**, each at its own address (`chat.dotrino.com`,
`here.dotrino.com`, and so on). There is nothing to sign up for and nobody to ask:
you open it and use it.

If you like one, you can **install** it so it sits on your home screen with its own
icon and opens without a browser bar.

## Android and desktop

1. Open the app in your browser.
2. Tap **Install app** in the top bar (it shows up when the browser allows it).
3. Confirm. The app now behaves like any other.

## iPhone and iPad

Apple does not offer that button, but it installs all the same:

1. Open the app in Safari.
2. Tap the **Share** button.
3. Choose **Add to Home Screen**.

## It works offline

Once visited, the app stays on your device: it opens again even with no connection.
Whatever you created is still there, because [it lives with you](/en/empezar/privacidad/),
not on somebody else's server.

## An Android app for the ecosystem

Some pieces are also packaged as an Android app for what the browser cannot do. The
ones that have it show their download button in [the catalogue](https://dotrino.com/).

The full catalogue lives at [dotrino.com](https://dotrino.com/) and its code, like
everything else, is in [`imdotrino/dotrino-home`](https://github.com/imdotrino/dotrino-home).
