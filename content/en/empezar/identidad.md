# Your identity and your profiles

The first time you open a Dotrino app, your browser **creates a key** for you. That
key is not requested, not registered anywhere, and **never leaves your device**: it
is what identifies you across the ecosystem. There is no username or password to
remember.

## What's yours comes along

Because the identity is the same, **your contacts and your things are the same** in
every Dotrino app you open in that browser. Start a conversation in one and continue
it in another without introducing yourself again.

## Your profile

You can give it a name, a picture and whatever details you want to show. It is
managed at [profile.dotrino.com](https://profile.dotrino.com/), and every app opens
it from the round button in the top bar.

You decide which fields are visible: phone and address, for instance, start hidden.

## Several profiles on one device

You can keep **more than one profile** in the same browser — one personal, one for
work — and switch between them. Each profile has its own key and its own things: they
never mix. When you switch, apps reload with the new one.

## If you want to go further

You can make **your own machine** the one that holds your master key and authorises
your devices: that is [the vault](/en/vault/modelo/). You do not need it to use the
apps — everything works without it — it is one more step for whoever wants it.
