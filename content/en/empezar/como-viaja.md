# How what you share travels

When you send something to another person, this is what happens.

## The shortest path

If the connection allows it, what you send travels **straight from one machine to the
other**. When the network does not allow it (office networks, some carriers), it goes
through an ecosystem relay that only forwards it: a courier, not an archive.

## Your message waits

If the other person is offline, the message **waits for them for up to a day** and
arrives the moment they open the app again. After that it is dropped.

## It carries your signature

Everything that leaves your machine carries a **signature born on your device**.
Whoever receives it can check that it came from you and that nothing changed along
the way. That signature is the same one that identifies you across every app: it is
[your identity](/en/empezar/identidad/).

## The links you share

When you share an app link — a room, a game, a transfer — what you share sits after
the `#` in the address. The browser **never sends that part to the server**: it
travels from the link straight to the person you gave it to, and it cannot be
searched or indexed.

Same idea underneath: [what's yours stays with you](/en/empezar/privacidad/).
