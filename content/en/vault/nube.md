# The vault in the cloud

Your vault usually lives on your computer or your phone, and when they are off nobody can reach
it. The **vault in the cloud** is the same vault, running on a Dotrino server: it wakes up when
one of your devices needs it and goes back to sleep on its own.

You do not need it to use Dotrino: everything works without it. It is useful if you want your
account to answer even when your devices are off.

## How much it costs

US$ 5 a month plus VAT (US$ 5.75 in total). It is charged with PayPal, renews every month and you
cancel whenever you want. You subscribe **from the browser**, at
[vault.dotrino.com/hosted](https://vault.dotrino.com/hosted): nothing is sold inside the phone
apps.

## How to subscribe

1. Go to [vault.dotrino.com/hosted](https://vault.dotrino.com/hosted) and press **Subscribe with
   PayPal**.
2. Approve the subscription on PayPal. It brings you back to the same page.
3. The vault takes a few minutes to be created. Leave the tab open.
4. When it is ready, the page shows an **address** (`admin@AB12-CD34-EF56`) and an **activation
   code**. Save them: the code is only shown in that tab.

## The first time you get in

Open the profile menu (the circle at the top right) in any Dotrino app, press **Sign in** and
type the address and the code. That browser becomes a device of your vault in the cloud. Change
the code to a password of your own as soon as you are in.

## Cancel

At [vault.dotrino.com/hosted](https://vault.dotrino.com/hosted), under **Your vaults in the
cloud**, press **Cancel**. It stays on until the end of the month you paid and then turns off.
**Nothing stored is deleted**: if you pay again, it comes back as it was.

If a charge fails, PayPal retries it. If it is still unpaid when what you paid runs out, the
vault turns off the same way, without deleting anything.

## What Dotrino keeps and what it does not

- What you store in the vault is **encrypted** on the server.
- Passwords and secrets are sealed for your devices.
- Dotrino knows you have a vault in the cloud and when it is used, because it wakes it up.
- PayPal gets the charge and an order number, nothing about your Dotrino account.
