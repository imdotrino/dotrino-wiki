# Dotrino Wiki

The documentation of the [Dotrino](https://dotrino.com) ecosystem, in one place.
Dotrino is an ecosystem of applications centered on the privacy of your data:
**what is yours is yours, and you decide what you share, with whom and when.**

## How this wiki is edited

Every page has an **"Edit on GitHub"** button at the bottom: the content lives as
Markdown in the [`dotrino-wiki`](https://github.com/imdotrino/dotrino-wiki)
repository and is published as static HTML by its own generator — no accounts, no
cookies, no third-party JavaScript. Changes come in as pull requests to `develop`.
